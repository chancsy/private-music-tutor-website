Feature branch · scroll-theme-transition

One page, three audiences.

Three explorations of a single page whose look shifts as you scroll — teens & students at the top, young children in the middle, working adults at the bottom. Same content in each; the difference is how the page transitions. Open one and scroll slowly.

Concept A

Morph

Colours, blobs and typography interpolate continuously with your scroll position — there is no "cut", the page simply becomes the next look. Light throughout, so text stays readable at every point.

JS sets two mix percentages from scroll; CSS color-mix() does the blending. Falls back to static section colours in older browsers.
Concept B

Chapters

Three full-screen chapters. The backdrop crossfades dark indigo → pastel → dark gold, and the top bar recolours to match. The most dramatic of the three.

Plain native scrolling; three fixed backdrop layers whose opacity follows each chapter's position.
Concept C

Stage

An illustrated keyboard stays pinned on the left while the audience panels scroll past; its props and colours change for each audience (headphones → stars and a smiling note → coffee and sheet music). On phones the stage becomes a compact sticky strip.

Sticky column + IntersectionObserver; every colour change is a plain CSS transition.
Shared across all three
  • Same three audiences and the same honest copy: credentials up front, a first-person note from the teacher, no hype.
  • All kids-zone artwork is original SVG — no licensed characters.
  • Placeholders are dashed-outlined, as on the main site. Photos are the same stand-ins (credits in each page's footer).
  • These are self-contained explorations; none of them touches the main site's CSS. Back to the main site.
